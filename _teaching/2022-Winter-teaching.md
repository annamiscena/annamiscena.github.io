This excursion takes students to Florence to explore the city’s extraordinary artistic and cultural heritage through a hands-on approach. Visits to major sites will provide students with the opportunity to engage directly with the works and spaces that shaped the Renaissance.

Visits include:

Battistero and Museo dell’Opera del Duomo;
San Lorenzo and Biblioteca Medicea Laurenziana;
Gallerie degli Uffizi;
Galleria dell’Accademia;
Museo Nazionale del Bargello;
Palazzo Pitti.
