The aim of this course is to dissect the journal "Ver Sacrum", an early project of the Viennese Secession. Through visual and historical analysis of published issues (1989-1903), we will explore the anatomy of a modern magazine: its creation, editorial history, readership, and its (premature) death. The course can be interpreted as a case study in the larger context of modern journals, providing a window onto print culture at the turn of the century and the innovative function it played for artists and readers alike. --- Contact hours: 22.5 (90'/week, 15 weeks)
