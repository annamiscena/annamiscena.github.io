I am a researcher and lecturer based in Vienna, Austria. My work is situated at the intersection of aesthetics and perception studies. 

I hold a PhD in Philosophy from the [University of Vienna](https://crea.univie.ac.at/meet-our-people) and a MA in Art History from the [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk).

I am interested in the phenomenology of visual perception, empirical aesthetics and cultural histories of attention. Due to my professional background in the art market ([Christie’s London](https://www.christies.com/en/locations/london), [Dorotheum Wien](https://www.dorotheum.com/de/)), I have also developed a particular interest in connoisseurship and materiality.
