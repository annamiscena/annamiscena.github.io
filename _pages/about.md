I am a researcher and lecturer based in Vienna, Austria. My work is situated at the intersection of aesthetics and perception studies. 

I hold a PhD in Philosophy from the [University of Vienna](https://crea.univie.ac.at/meet-our-people) and a MA in Art History from the [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk).

I am interested in the phenomenology of visual perception, cultural histories of attention and histories of seeing. Due to my professional background in the art market ([Christie’s London](https://www.christies.com/en/locations/london), [Dorotheum Wien](https://www.dorotheum.com/de/)), I have also developed a particular interest in connoisseurship and materiality. 



Current activities
------
I am currently a post-doc researcher and lecturer at the department of [Art History and Art Theory / Gender Studies / Curatorial Practice](https://www.kunstuni-linz.at/universitaet/organisation/institute/bildende-kunst-und-kulturwissenschaften/kunstgeschichte-und-kunsttheorie-/-gender-studies-/-kuratorische-praxis) at [Kunstuniversität Linz](https://www.kunstuni-linz.at/) 


Previously this year, I was a visiting researcher at the [Center for Cultures and Technologies of Collecting](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/de/universitaet/fakultaeten/bildung-kunst-architektur/departments/kunst-kulturwissenschaften/zentren/kulturen-und-technologien-des-sammelns.html) at [UWK](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/de.html).
